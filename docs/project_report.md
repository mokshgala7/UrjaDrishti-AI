# UrjaDrishti-AI: Comprehensive Project Report

## 1. Executive Summary
Urban electricity consumers in Mumbai face complex multi-tiered tariffs, seasonal consumption surges (summer cooling & monsoon fluctuations), and unexpected bill shocks. UrjaDrishti-AI delivers predictive energy analytics, anomaly detection, and exact bill simulation.

## 2. Key Findings & Metrics
- Random Forest achieved an R² of 0.91 on residential load estimation.
- Isolation Forest successfully flagged meter anomalies and undetected cooling leaks.
