# Mumbai Electrical Utility Scope

### Providers Covered
1. **Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited (AEML)**: Covers Suburban Mumbai.
2. **BEST Undertaking (Brihanmumbai Electric Supply and Transport)**: Covers Island City Mumbai (Colaba to Sion/Mahim).

### Tariff Details
- Fixed monthly meter charges
- Tiered energy slabs (0-100, 101-300, 301-500, >500 units)
- Wheeling charges (per kWh)
- Government of Maharashtra Electricity Duty (16%)
